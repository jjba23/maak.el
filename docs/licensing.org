* Licensing

maak.el, its tests, its packaging and its documentation, including every page of
this guide, are free software under the *MIT license*: use it, change it and share
it, keeping the copyright notice and the permission notice with every copy.

The whole text is in [[file:../LICENSE][LICENSE]], and every source file says so in
one line, so a file that travels alone still carries its terms:

#+begin_src text
SPDX-License-Identifier: MIT
#+end_src

** What that means in practice
:PROPERTIES:
:CUSTOM_ID: what-that-means-in-practice
:END:

- *You can use it anywhere.*  MIT is compatible with the licenses of the other
  Emacs packages you may be running it beside, and with the ones you may want to
  put around it — proprietary included.  Nothing here asks you to publish your
  own Maak files or your own Emacs configuration.
- *You keep your copies free.*  There is no warranty: a build is your build, and
  a task that removes a directory removes it.
- *Attribution is the one condition.*  Keep the copyright line and the permission
  notice; that is the whole of it.

A dependency is a licensing decision too, so this project takes one that is
needed, MIT compatible and pinned.
